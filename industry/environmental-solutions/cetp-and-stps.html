Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETPs) and Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs)

About the Industry

Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETPs) and Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) are at the forefront of sustainable water management. CETPs cater to the treatment of industrial effluents from multiple facilities, ensuring compliance with stringent environmental regulations. STPs handle municipal sewage, treating it to safeguard water quality and support community and ecosystem health. Together, CETPs and STPs form a vital part of pollution control and sustainable development.

Challenges in Wastewater Treatment

1. Complex Pollutant Load

Industrial and municipal wastewater often contains a diverse mix of organic, inorganic, and chemical pollutants.

2. Stringent Regulations

Meeting compliance standards demands advanced technologies and precise monitoring.

3. High Operational Costs

Energy-intensive processes and frequent equipment maintenance contribute to significant costs.

4. Scaling and Corrosion

Continuous operation can lead to scaling and corrosion, affecting equipment performance.
