Key Environmental Challenges in Tanneries

High Water Consumption

Tanneries require significant amounts of water for various processes, leading to concerns about water scarcity and resource management.

Effluent Discharge

The effluent from tanning processes contains harmful pollutants, including chromium, sulfides, and organic matter, which must be treated before discharge.

Air Emissions and Odor Control

Volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and odors from tanning processes pose challenges for air quality and workplace safety.

Solid Waste Generation

Byproducts like fleshing, trimmings, and sludge from effluent treatment plants need effective management to prevent environmental harm.

Regulatory Compliance

Tanneries face strict environmental regulations that require advanced systems for compliance without disrupting production efficiency.
