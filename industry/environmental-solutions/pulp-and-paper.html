About the Industry

The pulp and paper industry plays a critical role in our daily lives, producing essential products while focusing on sustainability and innovation. Our mission is to provide eco-friendly solutions for a greener future. The industry has evolved significantly over the years, adopting advanced technologies and processes to enhance production efficiency and reduce environmental footprints. It produces a wide variety of paper products, including packaging materials, office supplies, tissue products, and more.
