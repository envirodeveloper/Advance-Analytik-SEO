Key Environmental Challenges in Oil Refineries

Effluent Management

Refinery wastewater often contains hydrocarbons, heavy metals, and other contaminants, requiring advanced treatment technologies to meet discharge standard

Water Quality Management

Ensuring the quality of water used in processes and recycling is critical to refinery operations and regulatory compliance.

Energy Efficiency

High energy consumption in refining processes necessitates optimization to reduce costs and minimize environmental impact.

Regulatory Compliance

Oil refineries face stringent environmental regulations that demand continuous monitoring of wastewater discharges and water usage.
