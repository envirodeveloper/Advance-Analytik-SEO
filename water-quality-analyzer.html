Our Advanced Water Quality Analyzers

Advance Analytik® provides highly accurate and reliable water quality analyzers for a variety of applications, including online monitoring, laboratory testing, and portable water testing kits.

Parameters We Measure

  • pH & ORP Analyzer – Measures water acidity and oxidation levels
  • Turbidity Analyzer – Detects clarity and suspended particles
  • Conductivity & TDS Analyzer – Monitors salinity and dissolved solids
  • Dissolved Oxygen (DO) Sensor – Ensures proper aeration in water systems
  • Nitrate, Ammonia & Phosphate Analyzer – Identifies nutrient contamination
  • Chlorine & Hardness Analyzer – Ensures water treatment efficiency

Industries We Serve

  • Wastewater Treatment Plants – Monitoring effluent discharge quality
  • Drinking Water Facilities – Ensuring safe potable water supply
  • Pharmaceutical & Chemical Industries – Maintaining purity in production processes
  • Food & Beverage Processing – Complying with hygiene and safety regulations
  • Power Plants – Monitoring cooling and boiler feed water
  • Aquaculture & Fisheries – Ensuring optimal water conditions for marine life

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How does a water quality analyzer work?

A water quality analyzer uses advanced sensors to measure key water parameters, detecting variations in pH, conductivity, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, and more.

Q2: Which industries require water quality monitoring?

Industries like wastewater treatment, pharmaceuticals, food processing, power generation, and aquaculture rely on water quality analyzers to maintain safe and efficient operations.

Q3: Can your water analyzers support continuous monitoring?

Yes, our online water quality analyzers provide 24/7 real-time monitoring, ensuring continuous tracking of water parameters.

Q4: How do I choose the right water quality analyzer?

Selecting the right analyzer depends on:

  • The parameters you need to measure.
  • Application environment (industrial, municipal, lab, etc.)
  • Regulatory requirements for your industry
