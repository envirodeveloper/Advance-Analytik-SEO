About Us

Revolutionizing Analytical Technology

Agua Instruments Private Limited is a leading environmental solutions provider, now expanding its expertise to the UAE market. Headquartered in India, we extend our services across UAE in Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, and other Emirates, delivering cutting-edge environmental monitoring and process automation solutions. As system integrators and solution providers in Process Analytics, Process Instrumentation, Environmental Monitoring, and Emission Control, we specialize in delivering customized solutions tailored to the UAE’s regulatory and industrial needs.

Our expertise covers a broad spectrum of industries, including Oil & Gas, Petrochemicals, Power Plants, Water & Wastewater Treatment, Pharmaceuticals, Cement, Desalination Plants, Food & Beverage, Manufacturing, and Smart Cities. From ensuring water quality management in UAE to real-time air pollution monitoring, our innovative solutions help industries maintain compliance with UAE’s environmental regulations while optimizing efficiency.

At Agua Instruments, we are committed to providing sustainable, reliable, and technologically advanced solutions that align with the UAE’s vision for environmental conservation and smart industrial growth.

Our Products

Water Treatment Solutions

We offer high-precision water quality analyzers for pH, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, and turbidity monitoring. Our ultrasonic water level sensors ensure accurate measurement in reservoirs, tanks, and industrial applications.

Process Analytics Instruments

Our gas analyzers provide high-performance detection for process optimization and safety. We also offer reliable liquid analyzers for pH, conductivity, and total dissolved solids monitoring.

Emission Monitoring Systems

Our advanced CEMS and gas sampling systems ensure compliance with industrial air quality regulations, providing real-time monitoring of air pollutants.

Process Instrumentation

High-accuracy pressure and temperature transmitters, along with smart sensors, ensure critical process monitoring for industrial automation.

Automation & Control Systems

Our SCADA and PLC solutions enable real-time industrial automation and remote monitoring. We also provide IoT-based environmental monitoring for smart city solutions.
