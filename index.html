Analyzer

OPTICS-1000 Multi Parameter

The Optic 1000 Multi Parameters is a high-performance online water analyser that provides accurate and reliable measurements up to 7 parameters for a wide range of water quality parameters such as Ammonium, Nitrates, Nitrites, Total Nitrogen, Phosphates, Total Phosphates, Organic Matter [COD, BOD, TSS], Total Organic Carbon, Sulfides, Sulfites, Total Sulfur, Total Suspended Solids, Turbidity, Color, Chlorine, Chlorine Dioxide, Hydrocarbon, Aromatic Compounds, Phenol, Dissolved Oxygen, Conductivity, Ozone and pH. Designed with state-of-the-art optical technology, it ensures exceptional stability, low operating costs, and high precision for various industries.
